## How it works

1. Declares global variables `var counter = 0` and `var mu sync.Mutex`.
2. Multiple concurrent worker goroutines increment `counter`.
3. Encapsulating increments within `mu.Lock()` and `mu.Unlock()` guarantees serial access.
