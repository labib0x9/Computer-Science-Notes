## Concepts used

- Non-atomic increment operations (`counter++`)
- Data races on shared variables
- Lost updates anomaly

## How it works

1. Notes that `counter++` is not an atomic CPU instruction; it consists of read, modify, and write steps.
2. When two goroutines execute these steps concurrently, their operations interleave, resulting in lost updates and an inaccurate final counter value.
