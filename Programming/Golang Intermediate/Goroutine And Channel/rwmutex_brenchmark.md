## How it works

1. Creates worker sets executing varying percentages of reads vs writes.
2. Tests `sync.Mutex` where all operations acquire an exclusive lock.
3. Tests `sync.RWMutex` where read operations acquire shared locks (`RLock()`) and writes acquire exclusive locks (`Lock()`).
4. Tabulates execution duration across trials.

## Expected behavior (from reading the code, not run)

- Prints a comparison table demonstrating that when readers heavily outnumber writers, `RWMutex` dramatically outperforms standard `Mutex`.

## Things to watch

- Under write-heavy workloads, `sync.RWMutex` is slower than `sync.Mutex` due to the additional internal tracking and reader counting overhead.

## Terms

- **RWMutex**: Reader/writer mutual exclusion lock that allows multiple concurrent readers or a single exclusive writer.
- **Lock contention**: Delay caused when multiple concurrent threads attempt to acquire the same lock simultaneously.
